# Design System: Simplebooks

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Full Stop"**

Every headline on the site ends in an orange full stop. It is the system in one mark: the work is finished, filed, done. The rest of the language follows that promise of order. Navy ink is set on warm cream paper, in one confident sans (Plus Jakarta Sans at weight 800 for display, tracked tight). There is one hot accent, used sparingly for the thing to do next and the dot that closes a thought. The surfaces are quiet: cream, sand and white bands, faint navy hairlines, a soft grid that fades out behind heroes.

Density is generous on marketing surfaces (120px section rhythm on desktop) and tightens predictably at each breakpoint. Cards are soft rectangles with large radii and long, low, navy-tinted shadows that appear on hover; they lift a few pixels rather than glow. Motion is a single easing curve (an expo-out) applied to reveals, lifts and accordions, and it all collapses under reduced motion.

The client asked for less colour, so the system is deliberately narrow: navy, orange, cream and two neutral washes. Blue, green and violet card tints, guilloche, seals and rosettes were removed and stay out.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Navy ink (#0a1233) on cream (#fbfaf7); white cards; sand bands between sections.
- One orange (#ff5b2e) for primary actions, eyebrows and the full stop.
- Plus Jakarta Sans everywhere; 800 weight, negative tracking for display.
- Pill buttons, 22px cards, 12px fields; lift on hover, never glow.
- Sentence-case copy, no em dashes, every page ending in a consultation form.

## Colors

A two-colour brand (navy and orange) on a warm paper neutral, with grey-blue inks derived from the navy for secondary text.

### Primary
- **Registrar Navy** (#0a1233): headings, body ink on light surfaces, the navy button, dark cards, the stats bar and dark sections. Deeper variants (#060c24 for the platform and CTA bands, #050a1e for the footer and announcement bar) keep dark surfaces from flattening into one block.
- **Full-Stop Orange** (#ff5b2e): the primary button, the eyebrow label, the orange full stop, field focus halos, step numbers, icons and anything on navy. **Orange Ink** is the same hue deepened for small text on light surfaces (eyebrows, card numbers, links, asterisks, open menu items): full orange is 2.8 to 3.1:1 as text, Orange Ink is 4.6:1 or better on white, cream, sand, peach and mist. Dark sections switch Orange Ink back to full orange automatically.

### Neutral
- **Cream Paper** (#fbfaf7): the page background inside the v2 scope.
- **Sand** (#f5f2ec) and **Deep Sand** (#efece6): alternating section bands and quiet panels.
- **Hairline** (#e6e3dd): card, divider and panel borders.
- **Ink 2** (#3a4460): strong secondary text, list items, tags.
- **Ink 3** (#59637f): body and lead paragraphs.
- **Ink 4** (#646d88): captions, notes, labels, placeholders, review meta and stat labels. The lightest grey for any text on a light surface (4.6:1 or better).
- **Ink 5** (#98a0b8): non-text marks and illustration details only; as text it is 2.5:1.
- **Field Line** (#cbd1dc) and **Field Fill** (#f7f8fa): the edge and fill of every form field, firm enough to find on a white form card.
- **Card washes**: Peach (#fff5f0), Mist (#f3f4f8) and Paper (#f6f3ed) are the only tints a card may carry, plus white.

### Status
- **Filed Green** (#2ec48a): success and "done" status only (pulse dot, success tick, "Filed" chips). Never a surface or card tint.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** Orange marks the next action or the end of a thought. If two orange things compete on one screen, one of them is wrong.

**The Paper Palette Rule.** Card tints are Peach, Mist, Paper or white. No blue, green or violet tints, no gradients on cards.

**The Readable Grey Rule.** Text on a light surface is Ink 4 or darker, and small orange text is Orange Ink. Ink 5 is for marks, not words.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (with system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans

**Character:** One family carries everything: tight, heavy display headings that read like a confident signature, and an open, friendly body at small sizes. Self-hosted as a variable font (400 to 800).

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(42px, 5.6vw, 74px), 1.02): the homepage hero only. Inner page heroes step down to clamp(38px, 4.6vw, 60px), and split heroes to clamp(36px, 3.9vw, 54px).
- **Headline** (800, clamp(32px, 3.9vw, 52px), 1.06, -0.04em): section headings, always closed by the orange full stop unless the copy ends in its own punctuation.
- **Title** (800, 20px, -0.5px): card and step titles (17px in step cards).
- **Lead** (400, 16px, 1.65, Ink 3): the paragraph under a section heading; heroes use 17px.
- **Body** (400, 14.5px, 1.6, Ink 3): card copy and lists. Long-form prose is 16px at 1.8, max 760px.
- **Label** (700, 12px, 1.92px tracking, uppercase): eyebrows in orange; menu, plan and footer headings in grey at 10.5 to 12px.

### Named Rules
**The Full Stop Rule.** Every h1 and h2 ends in the orange full stop (`.v2_dot`), added by the component, never typed into the copy. Copy that ends in ? or ! keeps its own mark and gets no dot.

## Layout

A centred 1260px container with 40px gutters (28px under 860px, 22px under 720px, 16px under 360px). Sections stack as full-bleed bands, alternating cream, white and sand, with dark navy bands for the platform story, the CTA and the footer. Vertical rhythm is 120px per section on desktop, 96px under 1000px, 72px under 720px and 60px under 430px.

Section heads are either centred (max 680px) or a two-column split (heading left, lead right, aligned to the baseline). Card grids are 3-up (2 at 1200px, 1 at 720px), with 4-up and 2-up variants; three-up rows go straight to one column at 1000px so no card is orphaned. Split heroes put copy left and a framed illustration right, and reorder to copy, image, stats in one column under 1000px.

The desktop navigation collapses to a burger at 1200px; the mobile menu pushes the page down rather than covering it.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat at rest, lifted on interaction. Surfaces separate by tone (cream, white, sand) and hairline borders; shadows are long, soft and navy-tinted, and mostly appear on hover or on the one element that must float (a form card, the mega menu, a hero frame).

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card lift** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 60px -36px rgba(10,18,51,0.55)`): hover state for cards and plans, paired with `translateY(-6px)`.
- **Floating panel** (`box-shadow: 0 40px 90px -56px rgba(10,18,51,0.9)`): form cards, inline panels, embedded tools.
- **Menu** (`box-shadow: 0 30px 70px -32px rgba(10,18,51,0.45)`): the desktop mega menu.
- **Orange press** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 26px -10px rgba(255,91,46,0.85)`): the primary button at rest; deepens on hover.

### Named Rules
**The Lift, Not Glow Rule.** Interaction moves a surface up a few pixels and lengthens its shadow. No coloured glows, no outer neon.

## Shapes

Soft, generous rectangles and pills. Buttons, tags, chips, the region selector and the name-check bar are full pills (999px). Cards are 22px, mini cards and menus 18px, fields 12px, small nav targets 10px. Avatars and step numbers are circles. Borders are 1px hairlines in Hairline (#e6e3dd) or navy at 6 to 16% opacity. The homepage hero's scribbled orange underline is the one hand-drawn shape; no seals, rosettes or guilloche anywhere.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** full pill (999px), 16px 28px (small: 11px 20px), 15.5px bold, an arrow that slides 3px on hover.
- **Primary:** orange on white text with the orange press shadow. For "Start now" (to the dashboard) and consultation actions.
- **Navy:** navy with white text; secondary actions on light surfaces (Sign up, Talk to a consultant).
- **Ghost:** transparent with a Hairline border, navy text; turns white with a darker border on hover. On dark bands it switches to white text and a translucent border.
- **Hover / Focus:** lift 1 to 2px and deepen the shadow. Keyboard focus is a 2px Orange Ink ring offset 3px on light surfaces, white on dark ones, on every link and button (fields use their orange halo instead).
- **Disabled / Loading:** 62% opacity, no lift, progress cursor; the label changes to "Sending…".

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 22px (18px on phones and for mini cards).
- **Background:** white, or one of the three card washes; navy for the one dark card in a set.
- **Shadow Strategy:** none at rest; card lift on hover (see Elevation).
- **Border:** 1px navy at 8% (16% on hover).
- **Internal Padding:** 30px 28px 26px (24px 20px on small phones).
- **Numbering:** feature cards carry a two-digit number in a small white badge.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 12px radius, 1px Field Line border on Field Fill, 15px navy text (16px on touch screens, so iOS does not zoom), a visible label above every field with an Orange Ink asterisk for required. An unanswered select shows its prompt in Ink 4.
- **Focus:** orange border, white fill and a 4px orange halo at 18%.
- **Error:** a peach notice under the fields (`role="alert"`) that offers phone and email as the fallback; never a false "Thank you". Formats the API rejects (an email without a domain, a blank name, letters in a phone number) are caught at the field first.
- **Success:** the thank-you panel takes focus and is announced (`role="status"`).

### Chips and Tags
- **Style:** pill, navy at 5% on light (white at 75% on tinted cards, white at 12% on dark), 11.5 to 12.5px bold Ink 2.

### Navigation
- **Desktop:** 14.5px semibold navy items with chevrons; a white mega panel (18px radius, menu shadow) opens on hover (with a short intent delay when switching) or click, and a click on a hovered menu pins it rather than closing it. Long lists flow into two columns, headings in Ink 4 uppercase labels, links turn Orange Ink on hover, and the current page is marked (aria-current, a navy wash and an orange edge). Escape, a click outside or tabbing away closes it.
- **Mobile:** a burger that morphs into an X, opening a push-down panel of 16px bold rows with nested accordions (indented, with a rail), and Sign in / Sign up pills at the foot. Closed panels are inert (out of the tab order); Escape returns focus to the burger.
- **Region:** "Sri Lanka" is a static label, not a menu.
- **Announcement bar:** 40px, footer navy, one sentence and an orange "Start now" link.

### Page hero
Copy enters with a CSS rise (heading and lead move without fading, so they paint at once); everything further down uses the scroll reveal, which is skipped entirely without JavaScript or with reduced motion.

### The Full Stop (signature)
The orange `.` that closes h1 and h2 headings, rendered as a separate span so the copy stays clean and the colour stays brand-locked.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** end h1 and h2 headings with the orange full stop component, not a typed dot.
- **Do** use only white, Peach (#fff5f0), Mist (#f3f4f8) or Paper (#f6f3ed) for card fills.
- **Do** keep text on light surfaces at Ink 4 (#646d88) or darker, and small orange text at Orange Ink (#c3421a).
- **Do** send "Start now" to dashboard.simplebooks.com and consultation buttons to the page's own form.
- **Do** close every page with a consultation form or a clear next step.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use blue, green or violet tints, gradients on cards, or seal, rosette and guilloche motifs.
- **Don't** use em dashes in copy.
- **Don't** introduce a second accent colour; green is a status colour (a dot beside navy text), never text or a fill.
- **Don't** use Unicode glyphs or emoji as icons in new work; draw them as stroked SVG.
- **Don't** invent figures, offices or testimonials; the approved figures are 10000+ businesses and 1400+ Google reviews.
